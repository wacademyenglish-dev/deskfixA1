The Office

The Desk Prank

"Where is my desk? Who moved my desk?"

Directive 01

Welcome, Teacher

This is an A1 (Beginner) class.

We will use a classic workplace comedy scene to teach very basic, practical English. Keep sentences short and use lots of gestures.

Grammar Targets:

  • ❓ Question Word "Where": Asking for locations (Where is my desk?).
  • 📦 Prepositions of Place: In, On, Under, Next to.

Audio Logs

The Complaint

"That is weird. This is not funny."

Weird: Strange or not normal.

Funny: Making you laugh.

Notice the Demonstratives:

THAT (Far) vs. THIS (Near).

Audio Logs

The Command

"Calm down."

A very common command to tell someone to relax when they are angry or stressed.

🗣️ Speaking Prompt:

"When you are angry, does it help when someone says 'calm down'?"

Audio Logs

Kevin's Rule

"Wash your hands, Kevin."

A simple, hilarious hygiene command because Kevin was in the bathroom.
